About Me

Hi there! I'm Giselle van den Heever.

I'm a first-year Software Development student at CTU in Gqeberha, South Africa. I'm passionate about understanding how technology works, exploring programming languages, and developing my skills in frontend and backend development. I enjoy learning through practical projects and experimenting with new tools.

Outside of coding, I enjoy traditional and digital art, which allows me to combine creativity with my technical interests. I approach problems with curiosity, breaking them into manageable steps and exploring different solutions.

I've been working as a waitress at Mugg & Bean since 2024, where I've developed my communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills while building confidence in working with people. I've also gained workplace experience through work shadowing at Imbali Consumables and Services. My upcoming work shadowing at Wings Discovery will involve developing an educational application to help disadvantaged students learn.

I'm currently developing I WASN'T THERE, a narrative-driven game using Ren'Py and Python. This project combines my interests in storytelling, character development, visual design, and programming while helping me explore game logic and interactive experiences. I also enjoy experimenting with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build and customise websites.

My goal is to become a well-rounded developer with a strong understanding of programming fundamentals. I'm particularly interested in game development and cybersecurity, and I'm eager to keep expanding my skills through practical experience, personal projects, and continuous learning.
